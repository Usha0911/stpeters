Governing Body

St. Peter's Educational Association

St. Peter's College of Education is managed by St. Peter's Educational Association, a non-profit educational organization established in 1980 by Mr. N. Narayan Reddy. The Association has been committed to promoting quality education and social development across the Telangana region by establishing institutions that nurture academic excellence, moral values, leadership, and social responsibility.

Guided by its motto "Truth, Service & Joy", the Association strives to provide value-based education that prepares students to become competent professionals and responsible citizens. St. Peter's College of Education, established in the academic year 2002-03, continues this legacy by producing skilled, ethical and innovative teachers dedicated to nation building.
