Self-Paced Add-on Courses

Add-on courses provide additional learning opportunities beyond the existing curriculum, fostering skill development and knowledge acquisition. These courses enhance employability, offer flexibility in academic schedules, and promote personal growth by allowing participants to explore new interests. They encourage lifelong learning, professional development, exploration of emerging trends in education, familiarity with the latest technologies, and improvement of research and analytical skills.
