Two Year B.Ed. Programme

Academic Programme

The St. Peter's College of Education offers a Two Year Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) Programme designed to prepare prospective teachers with the professional knowledge, teaching skills, ethical values, and practical experience required for the teaching profession. The programme is spread over four semesters and includes Core Courses, Pedagogic Courses, Elective Courses, School Internship, and Courses for Enhancing Professional Capacities (EPCs).

During the programme, students receive subject-specific training in their chosen methodology along with hands-on classroom experience through school internship. The curriculum is designed to develop competent, innovative, and socially responsible teachers capable of meeting the demands of modern education.

St. Peter's College of Education offers the Two Year B.Ed. Programme with an intake of 100 students (50 seats in each unit). The optional subject-wise intake is as follows:

Note: A 10% reservation for the Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) shall be created over and above the sanctioned intake as per the guidelines issued by the Government from time to time.

Affiliations
  • Approved by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE).
  • Affiliated to Kakatiya University, Warangal.
  • Recognized by the Government of Telangana.

Admissions to the programme are made as per the norms prescribed by the affiliating university and NCTE. The curriculum is periodically updated in accordance with the latest teacher education regulations to ensure quality professional training.
