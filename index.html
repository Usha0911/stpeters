A long-standing educational association in Hanamkonda

St peter's Educational Association is a non profit organisation established in 1980 by Mr.N Narayan Reddy, promoting social and educational activities in Telangana region. It has established St. Peter's College of Education, St. Peter's High School, Day & Residential School in Vidyanagar, Hanamkonda and JMJ High School in Nakkalagutta, Hanamkonda. Its motto is "Truth, Service & JOY". They impart quality education with added values and develop skills to make the students of Warangal more competent in the fields of Arts, Science and Technology. St. Peter's College of Education (B.Ed) was established in 2002-03, affiliated to Kakatiya University, Warangal and approved by National Council of Teacher Education (NCTE), Southern Region, Bangalore. St. Peter's College of Diploma in Education (D.Ed) has been functioning from academic year 2007-2008.
