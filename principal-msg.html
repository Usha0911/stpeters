Principal's Message

Welcome to St. Peter's Educational Association. Education is the most powerful tool that shapes individuals into responsible citizens. Our institution strives to provide quality teacher education through academic excellence, innovation, discipline and value-based learning.

Our dedicated faculty members continuously encourage students to become competent professionals, lifelong learners and responsible members of society. We believe in nurturing leadership, creativity and service through holistic education.

We warmly welcome every aspiring teacher to become part of our institution and experience an inspiring learning journey.

Dr. John Peter
Principal
